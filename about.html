About Vikrant Iron

Rooted in steel. Driven by trust.

Vikrant Iron Private Limited is part of the Vikrant Group, carrying forward a long-standing commitment to steel distribution, product quality and customer relationships.

Our company

Built on experience. Focused on what’s next.

Established in 2003, Vikrant Iron Private Limited is one of the specialist businesses within the Vikrant Group. Its portfolio brings together steel and allied product categories for construction, industrial, agricultural and general-purpose applications.

As a distributor of Tata Steel products, Vikrant Iron connects customers with recognised brands and a broad product ecosystem.

  • Steel and building-material distribution
  • Wires, tools and allied product categories
  • Customer relationships across trade and project requirements
Our journey

A legacy that keeps moving forward.

Key milestones in the Vikrant Group's growth and the development of Vikrant Iron.

1985 · The Group's roots

Vikrant Group traces its beginnings to the entrepreneurial journey of its founders and its early presence in the steel industry.

2001 · Tata distribution partnership

The Group expanded its steel distribution business following Tata Steel's distribution model, with a focus on Western Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand.

2003 · Vikrant Iron established

Vikrant Iron Private Limited was established as the Group expanded its distribution footprint and product categories.

Today · A broader product ecosystem

The business is associated with a broad portfolio of Tata Steel product brands serving construction, industrial and allied markets.
